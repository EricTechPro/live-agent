# Tomato garden

Three raised beds behind the shed.

- Bed 1: Sungold cherry tomatoes. Bed 2: San Marzano. Bed 3: basil and marigolds.
- Water deeply every second morning in summer; bed 2 dries out fastest.
- Plant out after the last frost, usually mid May.
- Feed with tomato fertiliser every two weeks once the first flowers show.
