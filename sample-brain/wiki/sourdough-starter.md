# Sourdough starter

My starter is called Gus. Fed with 50 g rye flour and 50 g water.

- Feed Gus once a day when he lives on the counter, once a week in the fridge.
- He is ready to bake with when he doubles in about 6 hours.
- Before a bake, take him out of the fridge two days early and feed twice.
- Smells like nail polish? He is hungry: feed more often.
